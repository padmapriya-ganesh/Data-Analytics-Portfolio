# 📊 Project: Use Power BI to design a best-selling menu for a restaurant

### 📋 Overview
* **Domain:** Business Analytics
* **Role:** Business Analyst
* **Partnership:** Mentor-led Internship (“Menternship”) • The ThickShake Factory × MentorMind

---

### 🚀 Key Contributions & Methodology

* **Data Engineering (ETL):** Cleaned and transformed raw data using Power Query Editor, and built a Power BI data workflow, ensuring data consistency and analysis readiness.
* **DAX Formulas & KPIs:** Created DAX measures across 10+ KPIs including net sales, average sales, distinct bill count, confidence & lift.
* **Report Development:** Built an 8-page Power BI report covering product performance, sales trends, customer ordering patterns, and market basket analysis.
* **Business Insights:** Translated Power BI insights into a PPT-format business report, answering business questions & delivering actionable recommendations for menu optimization and understanding customer behavior.
* **Industrial Framework:** Developed a comprehensive CRISP-DM framework report tailored to the restaurant industry, covering stage-wise mapping, real-world use cases, case studies, and key business questions with relevant analytics tools.
