# 🍔 Project: Use Tableau to design a best-selling menu for a restaurant

### 📋 Overview
* **Domain:** Business Analytics
* **Role:** Business Analyst
* **Partnership:** Mentor-led Internship (“Menternship”) • The ThickShake Factory × MentorMind

---

### 🚀 Key Contributions & Methodology

* **Data Preparation:** Cleaned and prepared raw data in Excel and built a Tableau data workflow, ensuring data consistency and analysis readiness.
* **Metrics & KPIs:** Used measures & dimensions, and created Calculations & Calculated Fields across 10+ KPIs including net sales, average sales, confidence & lift.
* **Dashboard Development:** Built 8 Tableau Dashboards (incl 21 worksheets/visuals) covering product performance, sales trends, customer ordering patterns, and market basket analysis.
* **Business Reporting:** Translated Tableau insights into a PPT-format business report, answering business questions & delivering actionable recommendations for menu optimization and understanding customer behavior.
* **Analytical Framework:** Developed a CRISP-DM framework report for the restaurant industry, covering stage-wise mapping, use cases, case studies, common business questions & relevant analytics tools.
